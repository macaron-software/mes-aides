🇿🇲 Social Benefits in Zambia 2025

Zambia combines contributory NAPSA pension (40% avg salary) with non-contributory Social Cash Transfer (ZMW 400/month) for vulnerable households. Currency: ZMW (kwacha).

Questions fréquentes — aides sociales in Zambia

  • Who qualifies for the Social Cash Transfer in Zambia?

    The Social Cash Transfer (ZMW 400/month) targets the poorest and most vulnerable households, including those with elderly, chronically ill, or disabled members. Registration is done through local MCDSS offices. Source: mcdss.gov.zm.

  • How does NAPSA pension work in Zambia?

    The NAPSA pension provides 40% of average revalued earnings on retirement (age 55+). Contributions are mandatory for formal sector workers. Source: napsa.co.zm.

  • What is the Disability Grant in Zambia?

    The Disability Grant provides ZMW 300/month for persons with verified disabilities who are in low-income households. Applications through MCDSS district offices. Source: mcdss.gov.zm.
