🇲🇺 Aides sociales en Maurice 2025

L’île Maurice dispose d’un système de protection sociale universel avec la Basic Retirement Pension (pension universelle sans conditions de ressources), le NPF (pension contributive) et l’Aide Sociale (Social Aid). Monnaie : MUR (roupie mauricienne).

MUR 6 500 Basic Retirement Pension (60+) / mois
MUR 3 300 Social Aid / mois
MUR 5 000 Unemployment Hardship / mois

Frequently asked questions — social benefits in Mauritius

  • Who can receive the Basic Retirement Pension in Mauritius?

    All Mauritian citizens aged 60 and above are entitled to the Basic Retirement Pension of MUR 6,500/month. There is no means test and no contributory requirement. It is administered by the Ministry of Social Integration and Social Security (socialsecurity.govmu.org).

  • What is the NPF pension in Mauritius?

    The National Pensions Fund (NPF) is a contributory pension scheme: employees contribute 6% and employers 9% of salary. Retirement is at 60. The pension amount varies based on total contributions. Source: npf.intnet.mu.

  • How to apply for Social Aid in Mauritius?

    Social Aid (MUR 3,300/month) is means-tested for households unable to meet their basic needs. Apply at the district Social Security office with proof of income and assets. Source: socialsecurity.govmu.org.
