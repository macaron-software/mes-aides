Sociala förmåner Sverige 2026

Sverige har ett nordiskt (universellt) välfärdssystem med generösa förmåner finansierade av höga skatter och avgifter. Viktiga förmåner: A-kassa, Barnbidrag, Bostadsbidrag, Sjukpenning, Föräldrapenning. Valuta: SEK.

Vanliga frågor om sociala förmåner i Sverige

  • What is A-kassa unemployment insurance in Sweden?

    A-kassa (Arbetslöshetskassa) is Sweden's unemployment insurance system, administrated by independent unemployment funds (kassor) linked to trade unions. In 2026, you can receive up to SEK 25,000/month for the first 200 days (income-related, 80% of previous wage), then SEK 18,700/month for days 201–300.

    You must have worked at least 80 hours per month for 12 months in the last 12 months, and be a member of an A-kassa fund. Apply via your industry's A-kassa fund. The system covers employees and self-employed alike, but membership fees vary by fund.

  • How to apply for Bostadsbidrag housing benefit in Sweden?

    Bostadsbidrag (housing benefit) is administered by Försäkringskassan and available to low-income families with children, young adults (18–28) and pensioners. For families with children, the benefit can be up to SEK 3,200/month depending on income, household size and housing costs.

    Apply online at forsakringskassan.se. Income must be below approximately SEK 310,000/year for a family, and housing costs must fall within defined limits. The benefit is recalculated annually and reconciled against your final tax assessment.

  • What child benefits exist in Sweden?

    Sweden provides Barnbidrag (universal child allowance) of SEK 1,250/month per child under 16, paid by Försäkringskassan without means-testing. Families with 3+ children receive additional Flerbarnstillägg. Barnbidrag is automatic once the birth is registered — no application needed.

    Additionally, Föräldrapenning (parental allowance) provides 480 days of paid parental leave at 80% of income (capped at SEK 25,000/month) per child. Both parents can share the leave, with 90 days reserved for each parent.
