Häufige Fragen zu Sozialleistungen in Österreich

  • What is Arbeitslosengeld in Austria?

    Arbeitslosengeld (unemployment benefit) in Austria is administered by the AMS (Arbeitsmarktservice Österreich). In 2026, you receive 55% of your previous net daily earnings. The typical benefit ranges from €1,200 to €2,200/month depending on your salary.

    You must have worked and contributed for at least 52 weeks in the last 2 years (for first-time claimants). Benefits last up to 28 weeks initially, extendable to 52 weeks with additional contributions. Register at your local AMS office or online at ams.at.

  • How to apply for housing benefit in Austria?

    Housing benefits (Wohnbeihilfe) in Austria are administered by the 9 provinces (Bundesländer), not the federal government, so amounts and criteria vary significantly. In Vienna, the Wohnbeihilfe can provide up to €300/month for low-income renters.

    Apply at your provincial housing authority (Wohnbauförderungsstelle). Additionally, the federal Mietzinsbeihilfe helps with rent costs. Social housing (Gemeindewohnungen) in Vienna covers about 60% of the rental market.

  • What child benefits exist in Austria?

    Austria's Familienbeihilfe is a universal monthly payment from the Finanzamt. In 2026, the amount is €136.40/month for children under 3, rising to €165.10/month for children 19-25. The benefit is paid for children up to age 18 (or 24 if in education). All children resident in Austria qualify.

    The Kinderbetreuungsgeld (childcare allowance) provides €33.88/day (flat rate) or 80% of net income for up to 365/426 days of parental leave.
