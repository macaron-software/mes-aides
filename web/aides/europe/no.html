Norge har et nordisk velferdsystem med NAV som sentral administrasjon. Viktige ytelser: Dagpenger arbeidsløshet, Sosialhjelp, Barnetrygd, Bostøtte, Alderspensjon, Sykepenger. Valuta: NOK.

Modèle social : Nordique (NAV universel + cotisations élevées). Le NAV gère l'ensemble des prestations sociales en Norvège. Le système est financé par les cotisations sociales (trygdeavgift) et l'impôt. Montants en NOK 2026 (G = NOK 124 028).

Vanlige spørsmål om sosiale ytelser i Norge

  • What is Dagpenger unemployment benefit in Norway?

    Dagpenger (daily unemployment benefit) is Norway's unemployment insurance, administered by NAV (Arbeids- og velferdsetaten). In 2026, you receive 62.4% of your previous income, with a maximum of NOK 721,000/year (6G, where G=NOK 124,028 — the National Insurance base amount).

    To qualify, you must have had income of at least 1.5G (NOK 186,042) in the last 12 months, or 3G in the last 3 years. Benefits last up to 104 weeks (2 years) depending on previous income level. Register at nav.no.

  • How to apply for housing benefit (Bostøtte) in Norway?

    Bostøtte (housing benefit) is administered by Husbanken and the municipalities. In 2026, the maximum monthly benefit is approximately NOK 4,200. To qualify, your income must be below NOK 246,000/year for a single person, and your housing costs must be within defined limits.

    You must live in your own home (rented or owned) as your primary residence. Apply online at husbanken.no. Benefits are recalculated every 3 months based on actual income and housing costs.

  • What child benefits exist in Norway?

    Norway's Barnetrygd (child benefit) is a universal monthly payment administered by NAV. In 2026, the amount is NOK 1,766/month per child under 18 years old. It is not means-tested. Single parents receive double Barnetrygd for one child.

    Additionally, Engangsstønad provides a lump sum of NOK 92,648 at birth for parents not entitled to foreldrepenger. Foreldrepenger (parental pay) covers 100% of income (capped at 6G) for up to 49 weeks per child.
