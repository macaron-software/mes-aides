Usein kysytyt kysymykset Suomen sosiaalietuuksista

  • What is Peruspäiväraha unemployment benefit in Finland?

    Peruspäiväraha (basic unemployment allowance) is Finland's flat-rate unemployment benefit, administered by Kela (Kansaneläkelaitos). In 2026, it is €37.21/day (approximately €800/month). You must have worked at least 26 weeks in the last 28 months to qualify.

    For higher earners who are members of an unemployment fund (kassas), Ansiosidonnainen päiväraha (earnings-related allowance) provides up to €1,800/month. Both benefits last up to 500 days (approximately 2 years). Apply at kela.fi immediately after becoming unemployed.

  • How to apply for housing allowance (Asumistuki) in Finland?

    Asumistuki (general housing allowance) is administered by Kela for low-income renters. The benefit covers 80% of eligible housing costs above a minimum threshold. In 2026, the maximum benefit is approximately €600/month in the Helsinki area.

    Single persons can receive the allowance if income is below approximately €15,000/year. Apply at kela.fi or at a Kela office. Students have a separate student housing allowance (opintotuki asumislisä) administered alongside their study grant.

  • What child benefits exist in Finland?

    Finland's Lapsilisä (child benefit) is a universal monthly payment from Kela for each child under 17 years old. In 2026, the amount is €100/month for the first child, rising to €173/month for the fifth and subsequent children. It is not means-tested and is paid automatically once you register the birth. Single parents receive a supplement.

    Additionally, Äitiysavustus provides a maternity grant (baby box or €170 cash) at birth. Vanhempainpäiväraha (parental allowance) covers parental leave at 80% of income for the first portion of leave, with a guaranteed minimum of €37/day.
